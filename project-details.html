WMS Cloud Design Systems User Research WCAG 2.1 AA Mobile Barcode UI

The Challenge

High cognitive friction and input delays were throttling fulfillment in high-throughput distribution hubs.

Floor operators were navigating fragmented interfaces on rugged handheld Android scanners while warehouse managers struggled with delayed batch manifests on outdated desktop dashboards. Pickers were making costly mis-scans due to poor contrast in dimly lit aisles, and onboarding seasonal workers took more than 5 days.

The Solution & Impact

A unified ergonomic design system connecting handheld scanners and executive dispatch consoles.

1. Glanceable Scanner UI: Engineered high-contrast dark/light modes adhering to WCAG 2.1 AA with large touch targets (min 56px) for gloved operations and audible/haptic feedback loops.

2. Figma Design System: Created 200+ reusable components using Auto Layout 5.0, variables for color/spacing modes, and direct TypeScript token exports to engineering teams in two-week Agile sprints.

3. Measurable Business Results:
• 32% boost in daily order picking throughput.
• 41% decrease in barcode scanning errors.
• Onboarding time cut from 5 days down to under 4 hours.
