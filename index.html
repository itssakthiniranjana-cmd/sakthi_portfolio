Who I am

I'm Sakthi Niranjana, a Senior UI/UX & Product Designer passionate about creating meaningful digital experiences that balance user needs, business goals, and visual excellence.

With 3+ years of experience, I work across web applications, mobile apps, SaaS platforms, enterprise solutions, and design systems. From user research and wireframing to prototyping and high-fidelity UI design, I transform complex problems into intuitive, accessible, and user-friendly experiences.

Beyond product design, I bring a creative perspective through branding, visual design, and marketing collateral. I enjoy collaborating with cross-functional teams, exploring new technologies, and using AI-powered tools to improve design workflows.

My goal is simple: to design digital products that are intuitive to use, visually compelling, and meaningful to the people who use them.
