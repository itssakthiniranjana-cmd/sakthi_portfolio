ABOUT SAKTHI NIRANJANA

Designing With Purpose,
Clarity & Precision.

Sakthi Niranjana is a passionate UX/UI Designer, Product Designer, and Content Strategist focused on turning complex ideas into clear, intuitive, and visually arresting digital platforms.

She currently works as a Senior UX/UI Designer & Content Head at FlowHive Solutions (Atlanta, GA), contributing to product design, scalable design systems, and digital innovation, while also delivering high-fidelity UI and user research as a UX/UI Designer at GI TECH GAMING CO INDIA PRIVATE LTD (Chennai, India).

With a strong engineering background and deep expertise spanning mobile apps, SaaS platforms, and enterprise software, she seamlessly bridges product vision, design token systems, and front-end reality.
