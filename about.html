ABOUT SAKTHI NIRANJANA

DESIGNING WITH PURPOSE,
CLARITY & CURIOSITY.

Sakthi Niranjana is a UX/UI and product designer focused on turning complex ideas into clear, intuitive and visually strong digital experiences.

Her work spans UX/UI design, product thinking, visual design, branding, design systems and digital experiences across multiple industries.

She currently works as a Senior UX/UI Designer & Content Head at FlowHive Solutions, contributing to digital experiences involving WMS, supply chain optimization and AI-powered transformation.

With a background grounded in human-computer interaction, information architecture, and scalable design token pipelines, she bridges the gap between high-level creative direction and rigorous engineering implementation.

DESIGN MANIFESTO

GOOD DESIGN SHOULD FEEL OBVIOUS.

When an interface is truly intuitive, the complexity dissolves. The user achieves their intent without friction or cognitive strain.

UNDERSTAND THE PROBLEM.

Great design starts before the canvas. We dissect root user frustrations and business objectives before creating a single frame.

SIMPLIFY THE COMPLEX.

Transforming dense logistics, financial data, and multi-tier workflows into elegant, bite-sized cognitive steps.

DESIGN FOR PEOPLE.

Every pixel, micro-interaction, and typography choice exists in service of human clarity, accessibility, and delight.

BUILD WITH PURPOSE.

Aesthetic beauty and business impact are not mutually exclusive. Design with measurable intention at every stage.

ITERATE WITHOUT EGO.

True product mastery comes from continuous user observation, rapid feedback cycles, and relentless refinement.
